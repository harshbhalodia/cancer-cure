# AshaDeep (આશાદીપ) – Privacy Policy

Publisher: Xplore Horizons Inc. • Contact: xplorehorizons.in@gmail.com • Effective: 26 Nov 2026

AshaDeep is a gentle, family-themed casual game with devotional music and Gujarati voice interaction.

**What we collect**
- **Anonymous usage statistics** via Google Analytics 4: app opens, play minutes, level reached, voice command type (e.g. "pause"), and an optional mood tap (😊/🙂/😔). Each install has a random ID. We do **not** collect name, phone number, contacts, location, photos or health records.
- **Game progress** (level, settings) is stored only on your device.

**Microphone**
Used only when you tap the 🎤 button, to understand short voice commands (start, pause, slow, volume, music, theme). Speech is processed by your device's speech service (e.g. Google). AshaDeep does not record or store audio.

**Sharing**
Anonymous statistics are processed by Google Analytics. We do not sell data. If the app is provided by a hospital, the same anonymous statistics may be shared with that hospital to improve the experience.

**Children** – The app is not directed at children under 13.

**Deletion** – Uninstalling removes all on-device data. To request deletion of analytics data, email us with the approximate install date.

**Medical disclaimer** – AshaDeep is for relaxation and entertainment only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.
